About Clarion

Built for the angler, by the angler.

I grew up fishing native trout streams in the Appalachian mountains with whatever rod I could afford. One day I fished a buddy's rod, built specifically for the bait I was throwing, and started outfishing myself — same water, same bait, same hands.

That's when I learned a rod isn't just a stick with guides on it. Every inch of it is a decision, and most of those decisions get made in a hurry, on an assembly line, by someone who's never fished it.

I started building my own rods in 2018 — dialed to my exact cast, my exact presentation, my exact hands. Not because I wanted a hobby, but because I couldn't ignore how much precision was being skipped just to hit a price point. When a rod is built right, down to the smallest detail, you feel it before you even cast. It becomes an extension of your arm instead of something you're fighting.

That's the standard every Clarion rod is held to. Built one at a time, by hand, with the same obsessive attention to detail I'd want if I were the one holding it on the water. Because I am.

If I won't fish it, I won't build it.

Benjamin D. Founder & builder, Clarion Tackle Co.
