Alconite vs. SiC rings
Alconite is hard, smooth, and honest — it will outlast most anglers. SiC is harder still and sheds heat faster, which matters when a fish is burning line off a locked-down drag.
Stainless vs. titanium frames
Stainless holds its shape and shrugs off abuse. Titanium weighs less, flexes instead of bending, and springs back — you feel it in tip recovery and in how the rod sits in your hand all day.
Single-foot vs. double-foot
Single-foot guides keep the blank livelier and lighter. Double-foot guides spread load and take more punishment — the right call under heavy drag or where a rod gets thrown in a rod locker.
